# ContextBar v0.7.5

A menubar popover layout fix.

## Fixed

- **Popover cards shifted left and clipped, with gaps at the top and right.**
  `hasFullSizeContent = true` made the content fill the whole popover frame —
  pushing it under the arrow (the top band) and into the rounded corners (left
  edge clipped, uneven right gap). Letting NSPopover inset the content itself
  restores symmetric left/right margins and clears the arrow, so the cards sit
  flush and centered. Verified against a real popover capture.
