# ContextBar v0.7.2

A bigger Stats tab, a clearer "Value" tab and tidier settings.

## Added

- **Full-year activity heatmap** in Stats — a GitHub-style contribution graph
  with month/weekday labels and a Less→More legend. Hover a day for its tokens;
  click to drill in.
- **Daily / date-range breakdown** — pick a day or range (or click a heatmap
  cell) to see total tokens, sessions and estimated cost, with a per-project
  bar breakdown of where they went.
- **Insights** — a local, always-on section explaining your usage in plain
  language: a token-composition bar (input / output / cache-read) and narrative
  cards for sub-agent share, cache dominance + savings, context pressure, model
  mix, project concentration and your most-active day. Optional one-click
  "Analyze with AI" reuses the bring-your-own-key advisor (privacy-safe
  aggregates only).

## Changed

- Renamed the **"Cost" tab to "Value"** — the number is hypothetical
  pay-per-token API value, not a bill.
- **Consolidated settings** to five tabs (Usage · Stats · Value · General ·
  Privacy): Appearance moved under General, About under Privacy.
- **Refreshed section headers** with SF Symbol anchors and an optional ⓘ info
  popover, keeping panes scannable.
